ReSCUEApp provides a user-friendly interface for accessing and comparing seabird flight height distributions across UK waters. Whether you're an offshore wind developer, environmental consultant, conservation body, or academic, you can use this tool to:

- **Explore flight height data** for different seabird species across UK sea areas
- **Access recommended distributions** for collision risk modelling
- **Compare multiple distributions** to understand variations by method, season, and location
- **Explore flight risk scenarios** by adjusting air-gap parameters
- **Download outputs** ready for use in collision risk models
