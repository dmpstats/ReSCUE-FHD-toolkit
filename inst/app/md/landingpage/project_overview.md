The **Reducing Seabird Collisions Using Evidence (ReSCUE)** project is a multi-year initiative funded by The Crown Estate's Offshore Wind Evidence and Change (OWEC) Programme. 

It aims to improve our understanding of seabird flight heights to better assess and mitigate collision risks with offshore wind turbines.

**ReSCUEApp** was developed to simplify flight-height assessment for developers, consultants, academics, regulators, and anyone required to handle flight-height data.
